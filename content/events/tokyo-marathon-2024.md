On March 3, 2024, I completed the Tokyo Marathon.

The experience reinforced a simple lesson: ambitious goals become manageable
when they are broken into clear, repeatable steps. Progress is rarely dramatic;
it is built through preparation, consistency, and the decision to keep moving
when the work becomes difficult.

That lesson applies beyond running. Research, technical problem-solving, and
professional growth all depend on the same discipline: focus on the next useful
action, learn from feedback, and finish what you start.
