My academic path began with Japanese-language study and a Bachelor of
Engineering in Applied Chemistry at Tokai University. Engineering taught me to
break complex systems into parts, test assumptions, and work methodically from
evidence.

After graduating in 2022, I expanded into international relations and policy
research. I studied at Sophia University, worked in international relations at
ETUT National University, and later joined Waseda University as a MEXT research
student.

At Waseda, I completed a master’s degree in Policy Sciences. My thesis used
panel-data econometrics to examine export-market diversification and economic
volatility in natural-gas-exporting countries.

Alongside graduate study, I joined Apple Japan. As a Technical Expert, I
troubleshoot hardware and software issues, explain technical findings and
repair options clearly, and share knowledge with customers and colleagues.

The common thread is structured problem-solving: observe carefully, identify
the relevant evidence, test assumptions, and communicate a practical
conclusion.
