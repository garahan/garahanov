### Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of engineering, econometrics, and policy science. Key areas include:

- **Mathematical Modeling for Policy Prediction** — Using large-scale data with Python and Stata to model and forecast policy outcomes
- **Digital Twins & Process Simulation** — Creating virtual models of industrial chemical processes for training and optimization
- **Energy Systems Analysis** — Building on an Applied Chemistry foundation to analyze energy policy and sustainability
- **Quantitative Methods in Social Science** — Applying econometric techniques to social and economic policy questions
